Summary

Data-driven Postdoctoral Researcher specializing in marine macroecology and ecological modeling. I develop advanced empirical models to map global plankton distributions and predict ecosystem shifts. With extensive experience integrating complex, large-scale datasets, I translate ecological and biological data into predictive insights for environmental and climate applications.
